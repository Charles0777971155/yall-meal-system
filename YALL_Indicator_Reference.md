## Shared communities across projects

- **Kiliwu, Kponwansanyea-Kpeteyea, Kpayaquelleh New Town, Yowee, Tormue** — shared by Women's Rights, and now also the Women Agricultural Resilience Initiative (identical 5-community footprint).
- **Yowee, Tormue** — also part of Agroecology.
- **Yowee** — also part of Legal Empowerment.

Four separate grants now operate in overlapping ground in these communities. Worth keeping in mind for reporting to funders, so the same training or activity isn't unintentionally claimed as an achievement under more than one grant without disclosure.
